# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-03

Rhythia Reimagined is an unofficial community browser extension for Rhythia.
It is maintained by Shurielx and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Capo
Games.

This policy explains what the extension does with user data. Rhythia and Capo
Games operate their own services and privacy policies.

## Data and storage

The extension uses information available on Rhythia pages to provide its
features, including profile, score, and progression information. Data created by
the extension, such as settings, history, and statistics, is kept locally in
the user's browser.

The extension does not read or store browser cookies. Any account information
needed to request data from Rhythia is used only for that request and is not
saved by the extension.

The extension does not send user data to the maintainer, GitHub, analytics
providers, advertisers, or data brokers. It does not sell or monetize user data.

## Local backups

Backups are optional and disabled by default. When enabled by the user, they
are saved only in a folder chosen by that user. The extension does not upload,
synchronize, or share backup files.

Backups may contain personal information. Users are responsible for choosing a
suitable location, protecting their device and files, and deleting backups they
no longer need.

## Your choices and responsibilities

Users can manage and delete local data and backup files. These actions do not
remove information held by Rhythia or Capo Games.

The extension is an unofficial community tool. Users are responsible for using
it in accordance with the terms that apply to their Rhythia account. Capo Games
may change or discontinue its services, so uninterrupted operation is not
guaranteed.

## Questions and reports

Privacy questions and security reports can be sent to `shurieldev@gmail.com`.
Do not include sessions, cookies, private messages, profile exports, backup
files, or other sensitive information in a public issue or email.

Rhythia and Capo Games control their own server-side processing and privacy
practices. Questions about a Rhythia account or official records should be sent
to the official Rhythia support channels.

## Children's privacy

The extension is not directed to children. The maintainer does not knowingly
collect personal information through the extension.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when the extension's data handling changes. The
latest version is published in this repository.
